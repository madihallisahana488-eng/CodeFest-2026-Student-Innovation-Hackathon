A few ground rules

Keep it fair, collaborative, and focused on what your team builds.

  • Students from participating colleges can register.
  • Each team should have 2–4 members.
  • The hackathon runs for 24 hours on December 25–26, 2026.
  • Participants must provide correct registration details.
  • Projects must be presented by the registered team.
  • Teams should follow the event schedule and instructions.
  • Judges will evaluate projects based on innovation, usefulness, implementation and presentation.
